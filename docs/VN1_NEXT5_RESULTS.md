The concentration statistics are **descriptive**. The top-error rank uses
outcomes and therefore must NOT be used to select individual future series.

## 3. Higher-volume residual specialist (implemented; full benchmark in progress)

\`vn1_next5_research.py\` trains a standalone L1 LightGBM regression on
**realized units minus history-only statistical baseline** for training
observations that were active/high-volume at the historical origin.
Eligible series are identified from the last 13 observed weeks, with
mean ≥2 units and active share ≥0.5. At prediction, bounded residual
forecasts blend 20% or 35% with the learned-activity control, only on
historically eligible series. Weekly aggregate forecast units are preserved.

## 4. Adaptive seasonal ensemble (implemented; full benchmark in progress)

Compares historical AMSI and statistical forecasts for the **previous
13 completed weeks**, using only data available by the present origin.
The resulting error-proxy advantage determines modest 0–25% AMSI blending
at the series level, or hierarchically smoothed by client-warehouse group.
This is an imperfect historical proxy, not an actual past learned-activity
forecast; it is tested rather than assumed to help.

## 5. Expanded rolling-origin validation (implemented; full benchmark in progress)

Train on **five fully observed Phase 0 origins: 92, 105, 118, 131, 144**,
then validate exactly once on holdout origin **157**.
Winner chosen by pooled training official score only. A promotion gate
requires ≥0.001 absolute improvement on both pooled training and holdout
and improvement on at least **3/5 individual training folds**.
Training labels/feature dates remain strictly prior to the forecast
origin, and Phase 1/2 labels never enter model fitting or selection.
